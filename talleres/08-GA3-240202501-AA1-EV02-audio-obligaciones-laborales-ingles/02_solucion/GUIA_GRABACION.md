# Recording and submission guide

## What the rubric actually evaluates

The official product is an **audio** in English. The learner must demonstrate clear
pronunciation, rhythm, intonation, coherent ideas and correct use of `have to`, `must`
and `should` followed by a base-form verb. The introduction must include the learner's
name, identification number and training-group number.

No presentation, ZIP or synthetic audio is required. The learner's own voice is
essential because pronunciation is part of the evaluation.

## Before recording

1. Complete the missing local fields: `grupo_formacion`, `instructor` and `enlace_audio`.
2. Read `GUION_INGLES.md` until the ideas sound natural.
3. Use `GUION_PRONUNCIACION.md` only as practice support.
4. Confirm the audio format, maximum size and delivery method with the instructor.
5. Prepare a quiet room and place the microphone 15 to 20 cm from the mouth.

## Suggested recording settings

- Target duration: 3 minutes 10 seconds to 4 minutes.
- Speaking speed: about 95 to 120 words per minute, with natural pauses.
- If the platform accepts an audio file: MP3, mono, 44.1 kHz, 128 kb/s.
- If the platform requests a link: enable access for anyone with the link, without
  publishing the recording in GitHub.

## Recording sequence

1. Make a ten-second test and listen for noise or clipping.
2. Record the complete normal script with the learner's own voice.
3. Pause briefly between paragraphs; do not read the pronunciation spelling aloud.
4. Listen from beginning to end with headphones.
5. Check that name, identification number and training-group number are understandable.
6. Upload the recording only to the service accepted by the instructor.
7. Add the final URL to `enlace_audio` in the local profile and regenerate the PDFs.

## Meaning of the two local PDFs

- `ENTREGAR_<Nombre>_GA3-240202501-AA1-EV02_Guion_y_Enlace.pdf` is the only candidate
  for submission. While any required field is missing, it visibly says
  **BORRADOR — NO LISTO PARA ENTREGAR**.
- `APOYO_GRABACION_<Nombre>_GA3-240202501-AA1-EV02_Pronunciacion.pdf` is private
  practice material. It always says **NO SUBIR — SOLO APOYO PARA GRABAR**.

Never upload the personalized PDFs, personal profile or voice recording to GitHub.
