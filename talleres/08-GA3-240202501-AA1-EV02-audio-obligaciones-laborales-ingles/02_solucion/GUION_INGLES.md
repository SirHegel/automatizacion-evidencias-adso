# Responsible study and work in software development

## Purpose

This is the canonical English script for evidence `GA3-240202501-AA1-EV02`. It is
written for level A2.2 and is intended to be recorded with the learner's own voice.

- Verified length: 386 countable words in the public script.
- Expected duration: 3 minutes 10 seconds to 4 minutes at a clear pace.
- Required placeholders: `[LEARNER NAME]`, `[DOCUMENT ID]`, `[TRAINING GROUP]` and
  `[INSTRUCTOR]`.
- The public edition must preserve the placeholders. The local edition replaces only
  the data available in `perfil-aprendiz.local.json`.

## Script

<!-- SCRIPT_START -->

Hello. My name is [LEARNER NAME]. My identification number is [DOCUMENT ID], and my SENA training-group number is [TRAINING GROUP]. My instructor is [INSTRUCTOR]. I am a Software Analysis and Development student at SENA. This recording is for the activity Audio GA3-240202501-AA1-EV02. Today, I am going to talk about my academic and work responsibilities, good attitudes, and solutions to common problems.

In my academic life, I have to attend classes on time and complete activities before the deadline. I must read the instructions carefully because this helps me avoid mistakes. I also have to ask questions when I do not understand a topic. I believe students should practice English and should share useful ideas. In my opinion, a responsible student must respect other people's work and must not copy assignments.

In software development, teamwork is very important. Every team member has to communicate clearly and report progress. We must protect users' information and use strong passwords. We should write simple, organized code and test the program before delivery. A developer also has to document important changes so the team can understand the project.

Sometimes a teammate misses a deadline. First, we should talk calmly and ask about the problem. Then, we should divide the work into small tasks and agree on a realistic plan. We must not insult or blame anyone. I think respect and empathy are necessary because people work better when they feel supported.

Another common problem is an error in the application. The team has to reproduce the error, check the code, and test possible solutions. We should listen to different opinions before making a decision. However, we must choose a safe solution and explain it to the client. If we need more time, we have to be honest and give a new delivery date.

Good workplace attitudes include being punctual, patient, helpful, and open to feedback. I believe a good developer should continue learning because technology changes quickly. We must follow company rules, but we should also suggest improvements respectfully.

To conclude, students and workers have to be responsible for their tasks. We must act ethically, communicate clearly, and protect information. We should support our team and look for practical solutions. These habits can create a positive academic and work environment. Thank you for listening.

<!-- SCRIPT_END -->
