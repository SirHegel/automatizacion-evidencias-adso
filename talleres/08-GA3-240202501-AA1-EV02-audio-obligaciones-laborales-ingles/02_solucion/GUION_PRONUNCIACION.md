# Pronunciation guide for the recording

This guide follows the canonical script sentence by sentence. It is not a Spanish
translation and must not replace the normal English script.

## How to read the support

- Capital letters mark the strongest syllable.
- `/` marks a short pause; `//` marks a paragraph pause.
- `h` is softly aspirated; `sh` sounds like the beginning of *show*.
- `th` is made with the tongue lightly between the teeth.
- Names and numbers should be said slowly and checked in a short test recording.

## Final reminder

Practice by meaning groups, not isolated words. Record with the learner's real voice,
listen to the complete result and repeat any sentence that is not clear.
